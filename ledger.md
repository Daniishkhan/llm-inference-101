# Ledger

Every drill you attempt gets a row, on the day. The rules are in
[docs/methodology.md](docs/methodology.md), "Drills and the ledger".

- **Pattern**: fill it in *after* solving. Naming the pattern is part of the exercise.
- **Rungs**: the highest hint rung you used, 0 to 4.
- **Lookups**: how many times you opened the docs.
- **Runs**: how many times you ran the tests before they were green. Dry-run two inputs by hand before
  the first run. The aim is 1.
- **Result**: `green`, `green+hint` or `timeout`.
- **Redo**: 2 days and 7 days after the first attempt, empty the function (git keeps your first version)
  and type it again cold. Write the date and the minutes. A problem you cannot redo is not solved yet.
  A redo that falls on a Sunday moves to the Monday.
- **Katas**: a finished function of a done module, typed again cold, gets a row too. Write the set as
  `kata m01`.

## Readiness, each Saturday

Three numbers over your last ten new problems, worked out with Claude in Saturday's review. Redos and
katas are not among the ten. You are ready for a coding screen when the first number is 8 or more and
at least five of the ten had a time box of 30 minutes or longer.
